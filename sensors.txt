 The 15 simulated sensors:
 1. Weather (temperature, humidity, pressure)
 2. Sound (decibels with occasional spikes)
 3. Electrical (voltage, current, power)
 4. pH measurement
 5. ORP (oxidation-reduction potential)
 6. Conductivity
 7. Chemical concentration
 8. Spectroscopic (FTIR array)
 9. Dissolved oxygen
 10. Toxic gas (CO)
 11. Combustible gas (LEL)
 12. VOC (Photoionization)
 13. Level (fluid tank)
 14. Mass flow
 15. Load cell (weight with sine wave pattern)
